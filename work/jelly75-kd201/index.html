DESIGN

Sleek, versatile style

Every Jelly75 ships with a contrast-cut Esc key and spacebar that break up the deck at a glance. Pick Orange Meadow for a warmer, retro-leaning desk, or Volt Green for something louder — the soft white backlight underneath adjusts across four brightness levels either way.

2Colorways
4Backlight levels
CONNECTIVITY

One board, five devices

Pair it over Bluetooth, 2.4GHz, or plug in with USB-C — Jelly75 remembers up to five hosts and switches between them instantly, with dedicated keys for jumping between Windows and macOS layouts. Hold Fn+T to push polling up to 1000Hz wired or 500Hz wireless when input lag matters.

5Devices paired
2OS layouts
1000HzWired polling
500Hz2.4GHz polling
TYPING FEEL

Built on a gasket, not a tray

A full gasket-mount structure floats the plate on a cushion instead of screwing it straight to the case, so every keystroke lands softer and quieter. It's the same structure you can see straight through the smoky shell.

GasketMount structure
Uni-cushionVibration damping
PERSONALIZATION

Swap switches, not solder

Every socket on Jelly75 is hot-swappable, so switching from linear to tactile — or just trying something new — takes a switch puller and a few minutes, no soldering iron required.

5-pinHot-swap sockets
0Soldering required

Made to outlast the trend

Switches are rated for 50 million keystrokes, and the anti-slip PC keycaps hold their texture and legends long after cheaper boards fade. Charge it over USB-C and it keeps going for weeks on a single charge.
