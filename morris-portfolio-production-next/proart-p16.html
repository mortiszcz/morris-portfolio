The brief

How can a high-performance laptop feel like a composed creative instrument, rather than another piece of hardware?

Design intention

Power should be present, yet never compete with the work.

ProArt P16 was developed for creators moving between studio, location and collaboration. The design had to carry workstation capability in a form that feels calm enough to belong beside a camera, sketchbook or edit suite.

The product language brings functional zones, physical control and material changes into one readable surface. This case study focuses on the design decisions behind that clarity, from platform direction to the details that make the tool feel considered in daily use.

Platform direction

A quiet exterior for high-intensity creative work.

The system

Each surface resolves a different moment in the workflow.

FormClear volumes and measured transitions keep the product visually calm.
InterfacePhysical dial, touch surface and keyboard zones support fast, tactile control.
CMFNano Black and controlled metal detailing communicate precision without excess.

Result

A platform made to let creative work take the foreground.

The final ProArt language consolidates performance, portability and professional expression into a coherent product family. It is designed to be present when a creator needs control, and quiet when the work should speak for itself.

01 / TA RESEARCH

Next era of creativity
and AI productivity.

ProArt P16 was defined around experience curators: creators who need workstation performance to feel composed, immediate and portable across distinct modes of work.

02 / DESIGN FEATURES

Form follows function.

The physical layout turns thermal, interaction and product identity requirements into a single readable architecture. Each change has a structural reason, but the result stays visually quiet.

03 / NEW DESIGN

Island architecture.

Functional areas are divided in one CNC-defined landscape: keyboard, physical dial, sandblasting glass and trackpad sit in clear relationship without adding visual noise.

04 / MOCKUP VS. PROTOTYPE

Test it in full scale.

Mockups made the details tangible: edge radii, base thickness, vent geometry and the relationship between the display, chassis and palm rest were checked before release.

05 / CMF APPLICATION

Nano Black.
Ultra-black appearance.

A deep black colour and AFM-coated surface give P16 a focused, professional presence while resisting smudges through the long sessions it is designed to support.
